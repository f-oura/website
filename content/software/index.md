## Hadron Simulator

{{< lead >}}
Interactive hadron collision and decay simulation
{{< /lead >}}

Realistic-looking collision dynamics for illustrating baryons and mesons. Particles move, collide, and interact under simple conservation rules.

**Features:**
- Collision kinematics with momentum transfer
- Baryon and meson visualization with internal quark structure
- Adjustable particle counts and simulation speed
- Orbital motion of quarks within hadrons

## QGP Simulator

{{< lead >}}
Interactive Quark-Gluon Plasma to Hadronization Simulator
{{< /lead >}}

A visualization of the transition from Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP) to hadrons as temperature decreases. Quarks with color charges interact via gluon exchange and confine into baryons and mesons. This is a conceptual demonstration, not a hydrodynamic QGP calculation.

**Features:**
- Real-time temperature control (0.1 - 2.0 Tc)
- Visualization of color charge dynamics (R, G, B)
- Gluon exchange between quarks
- Hadron formation (baryons and mesons)
