### First Measurement of the \(K^-\) Escape Cross Section in the \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,p)\) Reaction

**F. Oura** *et al.* (J-PARC E42 Collaboration)

*Physical Review Letters* — accepted (2026)

[DOI: 10.1103/y6wr-rqjp](https://journals.aps.org/prl/accepted/10.1103/y6wr-rqjp) · [arXiv:2606.18398 [nucl-ex]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18398)

First measurement of the exclusive \(K^-\)-escape process in \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,p)\) at \(1.8~\mathrm{GeV}/c\). A simultaneous fit to the inclusive and escape spectra gives \(V_0=-72\) MeV and \(W_0=-100\) MeV at the nuclear center.
{{< /article >}}

### Cross Section Measurements for \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+\Xi^-)\) and \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+\Lambda\Lambda)\) Reactions at \(1.8~\mathrm{GeV}/c\)

**W. S. Jung** *et al.* (including F. Oura)

*Progress of Theoretical and Experimental Physics* **2025**, 091D01 (2025)

[DOI: 10.1093/ptep/ptaf064](https://doi.org/10.1093/ptep/ptaf064)

J-PARC E42 measurement of \(\Xi^-\) emission and \(\Lambda\Lambda\) production in the \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+)\) reaction, used to constrain the \(\Xi N\) inelastic interaction.
