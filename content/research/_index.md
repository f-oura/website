## J-PARC E42 / E88

{{< lead >}}
Study of kaon-nucleus interactions at J-PARC
{{< /lead >}}

The J-PARC E42 and E88 experiments investigate the interaction between kaons and atomic nuclei. These experiments aim to understand the behavior of strange quarks in nuclear matter and search for kaonic nuclear bound states.

**Key Objectives:**
- Measure kaon-nucleon scattering cross-sections
- Search for deeply bound kaonic states
- Investigate strangeness in nuclear physics

## RHIC-STAR BES-II

{{< lead >}}
Beam Energy Scan Phase II at RHIC
{{< /lead >}}

The STAR experiment at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) explores the phase diagram of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) through heavy-ion collisions. The Beam Energy Scan Phase II (BES-II) program systematically varies collision energies to map the QCD phase boundary.

**Research Goals:**
- Locate the QCD critical point
- Study the transition from hadronic matter to quark-gluon plasma
- Measure collective flow and fluctuations at various energies
