I work in experimental hadron and nuclear physics. J-PARC E42 remains the core of my completed analysis. I am also working on two-particle correlations in RHIC-STAR and on in-medium \(\phi\) meson mass measurements in J-PARC E88.

## \(\bar{K}\)-nucleus interaction {#kbar-nucleus}

{{< lead >}}
Exclusive \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,p)\) spectroscopy with HypTPC
{{< /lead >}}

The in-medium \(\bar{K}N\) interaction is strongly attractive, but the depth of the \(\bar{K}\)-nucleus optical potential is still debated. Inclusive missing-mass spectra constrain the real part only weakly, because they do not separate absorption from other processes.

In E42 I measured the inclusive \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,p)\) spectrum together with the exclusive \(K^-\)-escape channel \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,pK^-_{\mathrm{esc}})\). The escape-to-inclusive ratio is directly sensitive to the imaginary part \(W_0\). The first-author paper, accepted in *Physical Review Letters*, reports the first measurement of that escape cross section and a simultaneous fit of \((V_0,W_0)\).

This analysis was the subject of my doctoral thesis.

**Current focus:**
- Optical potential of \(\bar{K}\) in \(^{12}\mathrm{C}\) from inclusive and exclusive spectra
- Search for deeply bound kaonic nuclear states in the exclusive HypTPC sample

## \(S=-2\) systems in E42 {#s2-systems}

{{< lead >}}
H-dibaryon search and \(\Xi\)-nucleus interaction
{{< /lead >}}

E42 was designed as an H-dibaryon search in the \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+)\) reaction at \(1.8~\mathrm{GeV}/c\). HypTPC reconstructs \(\Lambda\Lambda\), \(\Xi^-p\), and related final states over a large acceptance, so the same data also constrain the \(\Xi\)-nucleus interaction.

I contribute to this program as a member of the E42 collaboration, including exclusive reconstruction, cross-section measurements of \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+\Xi^-)\) and \(^{12}\mathrm{C}(K^-,K^+\Lambda\Lambda)\), and related analyses.

**Related topics:**
- H-dibaryon search near the \(\Lambda\Lambda\) and \(\Xi N\) thresholds
- \(\Xi^-\) emission and \(\Xi N\to\Lambda\Lambda\) conversion in carbon
- Polarization of \(\Xi^-\) produced in \((K^-,K^+)\)

## Two-particle correlations at RHIC-STAR {#star-correlations}

{{< lead >}}
Femtoscopy and two-particle correlations in heavy-ion collisions
{{< /lead >}}

I am currently working on two-particle correlation measurements in the STAR experiment at RHIC. These correlations probe the space–time structure of the emitting source and hadron–hadron interactions in heavy-ion collisions.

## In-medium \(\phi\) meson mass at J-PARC E88 {#e88-phi}

{{< lead >}}
\(\phi \to K^+K^-\) in proton–nucleus collisions
{{< /lead >}}

J-PARC E88 aims to measure the in-medium modification of the \(\phi\) meson mass in cold nuclear matter through high-statistics \(\phi \to K^+K^-\) decays in \(p+A\) collisions, complementary to the dilepton measurement in E16. I am involved in this program as a current research topic.
